# Australian Employment by Industry

**Domain:** Economics
**Source:** ABS Labour Force Survey
**URL:** https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/employment-and-unemployment/labour-force-australia

## Description
Employment data broken down by industry sector across Australia. Covers full-time, part-time, and total employment figures by ANZSIC industry divisions. Includes regional employment patterns, employment-to-population ratios, and quarterly industry trends.

## ML Potential
- Industry employment trend forecasting
- Labour market analysis and prediction
- Economic sector growth modelling

## Files
- `raw/` — Raw ABS labour force data
- `processed/` — Cleaned industry employment tables
- `features/` — Engineered features for ML
