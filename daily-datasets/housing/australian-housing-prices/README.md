# Australian Housing Prices

**Domain:** Housing
**Source:** ABS (Australian Bureau of Statistics)
**URL:** https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation

## Description
Residential property price indexes and median house prices across Australian capital cities and regional areas. Includes established houses, attached dwellings, and total dwelling values. Sourced from ABS CPI data and Residential Property Price Indexes.

## ML Potential
- Housing price prediction and valuation
- Regional property market trend analysis
- Economic indicator forecasting
