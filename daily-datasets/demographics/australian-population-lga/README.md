# Australian Population by LGA

**Domain:** Demographics
**Source:** ABS (Australian Bureau of Statistics) — Estimated Resident Population (ERP)
**URL:** https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/regional-population

## Description
Estimated Resident Population (ERP) by Local Government Area (LGA) across all Australian states and territories. Includes population counts by age, sex, and year from ABS regional population data. Covers over 500 LGAs with annual updates.

## ML Potential
- Population growth forecasting
- Demographic clustering and regional classification
- Urban planning and resource allocation modelling

## Files
- `raw/` — Raw ABS ERP data files
- `processed/` — Cleaned LGA population tables
- `features/` — Feature-engineered demographic datasets
