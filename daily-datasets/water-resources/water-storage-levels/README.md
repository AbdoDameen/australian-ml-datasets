# Australian Water Storage Levels

**Domain:** Water Resources
**Source:** BOM (Bureau of Meteorology) — Water Data Online
**URL:** http://www.bom.gov.au/water/data/

## Description
Daily water storage levels for major dams and reservoirs across Australia. Includes storage volume, percentage capacity, inflow, outflow, and historical comparison data. Managed by the Bureau of Meteorology's Water Data Online service.

## ML Potential
- Water storage level forecasting
- Drought prediction and monitoring
- Reservoir management optimisation

## Files
- `raw/` — Raw BOM water data
- `processed/` — Cleaned storage time series
- `features/` — Feature-engineered datasets
