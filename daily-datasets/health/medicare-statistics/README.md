# Australian Medicare Statistics

**Domain:** Health
**Source:** Australian Department of Health and Aged Care
**URL:** https://www.health.gov.au/resources/medicare-statistics

## Description
Medicare benefits statistics for Australia covering services provided and benefits paid under the Medicare Benefits Schedule (MBS). Includes data by MBS item number, provider type, patient demographics, and geographical area. Tracks GP visits, specialist consultations, diagnostic imaging, and pathology services.

## ML Potential
- Healthcare utilisation forecasting
- Service demand prediction
- Health spending trend analysis

## Files
- `raw/` — Raw Medicare statistics data
- `processed/` — Cleaned benefit/service records
- `features/` — Engineered features for ML
